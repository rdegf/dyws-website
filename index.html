PROGRAMME

Eight days, following the rhythm of the bush.

  1. DAY 1

    Arrival & into the bush

    Land in Maun’s warm, dusty air, meet the group, and head straight into the bush to our first camp.

  2. DAY 2–7

    Into the Delta

    Rise with first light to track wildlife on foot as the land warms, and head out again in the golden hours. In between: guided reflection and somatic work in the shade, and nights in small tents, sometimes with the low call of lions in the distance.

  3. DAY 8

    Closing the circle

    One last walk in the cool morning air, then back to Maun for a closing ceremony to honour what you take home.

  4. DAY 9

    Departure & integration

    Fly home with clear commitments and the stillness of the bush still in you, followed by a 1:1 integration session and a group reunion.

THE PLACE

The Delta, where the wild still rules.

Botswana is a land of vast, sun-baked plains, endless skies and the quiet stillness of the Kalahari. In its north, the Okavango River spills into the desert and, instead of reaching the sea, fans out into a maze of channels, lagoons and islands: the Okavango Delta, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Africa’s richest wildlife areas.

It’s in this wild heart that our trail unfolds. Two hours north of Maun, we leave the roads behind for a community-run concession along the Gomoti River, renowned for abundant wildlife and predator activity. Our base camp is simple but comfortable: canvas safari tents with proper beds, set among towering fan palms. During the week, we also stay at two satellite camps deeper in the bush. Your trail directly supports the Kaziikini community that calls this land home.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before you say yes. Anything else? The discovery call is the place for it.

What kind of people join?

Participants come from many walks of life: leaders, entrepreneurs, professionals and creatives. Some are navigating a transition in their career or personal life. Others simply feel the pull to step away from the noise and reconnect with what matters. What unites them is a desire for growth, clarity, adventure and authentic connection.

What level of fitness is required?

You should be comfortable walking 5 to 10 kilometres a day and able to handle warm temperatures. A basic level of fitness and stamina will help you fully enjoy the trail.

What level of comfort can I expect?

A balance between comfort and adventure: not a luxury safari lodge, but not a self-sufficient expedition either. At Kaziikini Camp you stay in comfortable safari tents with proper beds. Deeper in the bush you sleep in smaller tents on sleeping mats, closer to nature. Your main luggage is transported for you, so there’s no heavy backpack to carry, and a chef prepares nutritious meals throughout.

What language will be spoken?

The trail is in English, unless all participants are Dutch, in which case it will be mostly in Dutch.

How structured is the programme? Do I have to join everything?

The itinerary is thoughtfully designed but leaves room for spontaneity and nature’s rhythm; we adapt to weather, wildlife and group energy. We encourage full participation, as each element supports the overall experience, but all activities are voluntary and we trust you to honour your own pace.

What kind of support can I expect?

You’ll be guided by our expert nature guide Sevara, supported by the NGS (Nomad Guide School) team and Rick. Someone is always nearby to help with logistics, comfort and care throughout the journey.

What about food and drinks?

Fresh, healthy meals that nourish body and mind, with dietary preferences accommodated; just let us know during your intake. The trail is alcohol-free, to create space for clarity, presence and connection.

Is there wifi or mobile signal?

During the trail there is no wifi and only limited mobile signal, so you can fully disconnect. You’ll have signal and wifi in Maun on the first and last day. For safety, we carry a satellite device for emergency communication at all times.

How do I get there, and when should I book flights?

From outside Africa, the best option is to fly via Johannesburg or Cape Town to Maun. Please arrive in Maun no later than 14:00 on Sunday 18 April and plan to depart no earlier than 13:00 on Monday 26 April; we ask everyone to commit to the full programme. Wait to book your flights until the trail is confirmed: we’ll let you know as soon as the minimum number of participants is reached.

What’s the minimum number of participants?

The trail goes ahead with a minimum of 4 participants. If fewer than 4 are registered, the trail may be cancelled or postponed, and we’ll let you know at least 45 days in advance. Participant fees are then fully refunded; we are not responsible for flight costs.

Can I book this as a business expense?

Yes. If the trail supports your professional or leadership development, we can invoice your business. Just email your company details to hello@discoveryourwildself.com.
